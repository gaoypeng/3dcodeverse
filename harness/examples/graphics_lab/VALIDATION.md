## Render artifacts

The retained gallery has **60 authored stills**, eight 6-second H.264 films at
24 FPS and 1280x720, plus a separate hearth film. Captures used the NVIDIA GeForce
RTX 5090 Laptop GPU through ANGLE/D3D12. Per-film `.capture.json` files record
144 rendered frames and zero console/shader errors. `render_ms_mean` is capture
instrumentation, not a certified realtime FPS benchmark.

The six library studies were visually inspected and revised. Actual encoded
frames show visible motion in fire, ocean, stream and meadow. Sand dunes and
rocks are static geometry studies; windborne sand motion is subtle at the wide
camera. The images are rendered from code with no generated bitmap backgrounds.
`output/overview.jpg` is a contact sheet of actual library-study frames.

## Actual coding-agent trials

The retained A1/A2 examples were generated by **GPT-6 Luna through Codex CLI**.
The initial courtyard failed to boot due to an undefined `THREE`; the initial
creek rendered with a raised water/bed ribbon. A second model invocation received
supervisor render feedback and produced revised source. Both revised modules
were copied unchanged into `agent_cases/`, then rendered and browser-tested by
the supervisor. The first invocation's local server bind was sandbox-blocked;
the second allowed sandbox networking for the local renderer. Prompts, hashes,
usage and review limitations are in [the case provenance](agent_cases/README.md).
These are direct CLI usability trials, not full harness plan/judge runs.

Three separate **Gemini full-pipeline runs** used `gemini-cli:gemini-3.7-flash`,
the matching planner, and `gemini:gemini-3.1-pro-preview` as judge. Each requested
one baseline plus one refinement with a 30-minute budget. Repeated provider
503s occurred. Each retained one baseline and ended with `status: budget` before
refinement completed; none passed baseline acceptance:

The trials exposed a planning ambiguity: standalone asset generation permits
only Three.js imports, while effects belong in zone/environment code. The final
scene planner explicitly directs those calls to the owning zone and avoids
rebuilding water/flame/grass as duplicate assets. This final prompt correction
has offline checks; it has not been validated by another paid pipeline run.

## Practical limits

These are realtime procedural effects, not combustion, fluid, granular erosion
or photographic asset reconstruction. Ocean reflection uses a mean plane;
stream clarity is approximate; grass density and shadows require an appropriate
budget. Close rock silhouettes, the Luna creek boundary and several large props
still show procedural construction. The six library studies and two agent trials
are labelled separately so curated work is not presented as autonomous output.
